# Project Context — Ray-Ban Customer Trust Intelligence

## 1. Overview & Academic Context
Developed as part of an HEC Paris Strategic Management course.
- **Company**: EssilorLuxottica
- **Focal Brand**: Ray-Ban (eyewear, heritage, brand trust)
- **Product**: Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses (camera/audio wearable AI glasses)
- **Primary Stakeholder**: Customers
- **Historical Research Period**: 2023–2026
- **Strategic Horizon**: 2030

---

## 2. Central Research Question
> **Is the privacy controversy surrounding Ray-Ban Meta remaining confined to smart glasses, or is it contaminating customer perceptions of the wider Ray-Ban brand?**

---

## 3. Strategic Hypotheses
- **H1**: Privacy controversies negatively affect customer perceptions of Ray-Ban Meta.
- **H2**: Negative perceptions spill over into the wider Ray-Ban brand.
- **H3**: Customers distinguish Ray-Ban's fashion identity from Meta's technology and privacy responsibilities, limiting reputational damage to the core brand.

*Note: These are hypotheses to test through empirical data, not foregone conclusions.*

---

## 4. Key Analytical Principles
1. **No Presumption of Damage**: The platform objectively tests for brand damage rather than assuming it exists.
2. **Distinguish Product vs. Brand**: A negative sentiment regarding Ray-Ban Meta is not automatically evidence of damage to wider Ray-Ban eyewear.
3. **Spillover Classification**: Distinguish between:
   - *Explicit spillover* (explicitly stating distrust in Ray-Ban brand because of Meta glasses).
   - *Implicit spillover* (negative brand sentiment correlating with privacy issues).
   - *No spillover* (privacy concerns confined strictly to the gadget/Meta tech).
   - *Unclear / neutral cases*.
4. **Attribution of Responsibility**: Track who customers hold accountable:
   - Ray-Ban / EssilorLuxottica
   - Meta
   - Both
   - Unclear / Neither
5. **Separate Customer Voice from Media Coverage**: Do not conflate public online discussions with journalistic media articles.
6. **Data Traceability & Evidence Grounding**: Every metric and AI finding must be traceable back to original text records, URLs, and publication dates. Never fabricate or extrapolate beyond verified data.
7. **Sample Representativeness**: Public online discussions represent an engaged, non-representative sample, not a direct proxy for all Ray-Ban consumers.

---

## 5. Target Platform Architecture
- **Executive Overview**: High-level KPI cards, health index, strategic summary.
- **Customer Voice**: Granular feedback, discussions, and sentiment exploration.
- **Brand Contagion Analysis**: Spillover tracking, attribution breakdown, contagion vectors.
- **Trends & Events**: Chronological timeline, privacy event correlations (2023–2026).
- **AI Intelligence**: Grounded synthesis, evidence-cited answers.
- **Strategy Lab**: Scenario simulations for EssilorLuxottica leadership towards 2030.
- **Data Collection Centre (Phase 2 Foundation)**: Ingestion, validation, cleaning, deduplication, and explorer.
