# Sourced context timeline: Ray-Ban Meta, 2023-2026

Reviewed through 16 November 2026. These are real-world context records, not customer discussions or a sentiment sample.

Purposive selection of accessible official announcements and independent reporting; short original paraphrases only. No full articles or customer comments stored. Dates distinguish announcements, scheduled availability, effective dates and reporting. Current page versions may include later edits. This is not an exhaustive incident census or a causal dataset.

Company announcements verify what was announced, not whether every performance or safeguard claim is independently true. Reported allegations and company responses retain their status. Product criticism and press attention do not establish wider Ray-Ban brand contagion.
